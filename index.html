Platform Features

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO DISTRIBUTE VIDEO AT SCALE

Smart Upload & Ingest
Upload video with full metadata, tags, timestamps, categories, and rights information in one streamlined workflow.
Multi-Format Delivery
Subscribers download clips in their preferred resolution, aspect ratio, and quality—4K, 1080p, 720p, and more.
Watermark & Branding
Apply custom logo overlays or watermarks before download. Protect content while enabling licensed distribution.
Advanced Search & Filter
Find content instantly with full-text search, category filters, date ranges, and custom metadata queries.
Timestamped Metadata
Rich shot-by-shot descriptions with clickable timestamps that jump directly to key moments in the clip.
Role-Based Access
Admin panel for content managers. Subscriber portal for licensees. Full RBAC with granular permissions.
How It Works

FROM UPLOAD TO DOWNLOAD IN MINUTES

01
Admin Uploads Content
Content managers upload video files with full metadata—title, description, categories, tags, and shot-by-shot timestamps.
02
Subscribers Browse the Feed
Licensed subscribers scroll a curated feed organized by category—Sports, Politics, Entertainment, Breaking News, and more.
03
Find & Preview
Use search and filters to pinpoint exactly the right clip. Click timestamps in the description to jump to key moments.
04
Download with Branding
Choose resolution, format, and aspect ratio. Optionally apply a logo watermark before downloading the final file.
